# Privacy Policy — Zen Petals

*Effective date: [launch date]. This policy covers the Zen Petals mobile game ("the Game") published by PetalAxe Studios.*

## The short version
Zen Jigsaw works entirely on your device. We do not have servers, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Your puzzle progress, imported photos, and music files never leave your phone.

## What the Game stores — and where
| Data | Where it lives | Ever sent anywhere? |
|---|---|---|
| Puzzle progress, best times, streaks, settings | Your device's app storage (localStorage / app data) | No |
| Photos you import for custom puzzles | Your device only, processed in memory on-device | No |
| Music files you import | Your device only, played locally | No |
| Save-game backups (auto-resume) | Your device only | No |

Uninstalling the Game permanently deletes all of the above. Clearing app data does the same.

## Advertising
The store version of the Game displays ads served by Google AdMob. When ads are enabled:
- Google and its partners may use the **advertising ID** of your device to show relevant ads, and may collect standard diagnostic data (IP address approximated, device model, OS version).
- We do not control and do not receive this data. Google's practices are explained at **policies.google.com/technologies/ads** and **google.com/intl/en_in/policies/privacy**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID anytime in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- Rewarded ads are always optional — every feature can be used without watching them.

## Children
The Game contains no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no data collection. It is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Permissions
The Game requests **no permissions** at install. Photo/music import uses the system file picker on demand — files are read only for the item you select and are never copied elsewhere.
